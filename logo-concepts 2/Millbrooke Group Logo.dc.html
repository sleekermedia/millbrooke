Millbrooke Group — Logo Exploration 01

Sunrise over water, made corporate.

Four refinements of the client draft. Each keeps the sun-and-water idea but replaces hand-drawn strokes with consistent geometry, a single brand navy with steel-blue accent, and the Montserrat wordmark the current brand already uses.
