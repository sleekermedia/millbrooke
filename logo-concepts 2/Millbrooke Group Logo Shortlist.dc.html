Millbrooke Group — Logo Exploration 05

The client draft, redrawn.

The same composition as the draft: the wordmark runs out from the sun along the horizon, with rays above and water below. The rays are now precise tapered wedges at even angles, and the waves are identical, evenly spaced and cut cleanly to the circle. For icons, the mark closes up into a full symmetrical sun.
